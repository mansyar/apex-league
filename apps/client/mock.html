Motion notes

  • Entrance settle — clusters slide in from their anchor edge with a slight overshoot, staggered ~60ms (score bug → lower-third → dock), on load and screen returns.
  • AP spend — the spent pip flashes out (scale + fade), the remainder re-seat. Bonus pips (cup win) cut in with a chevron notch and glow once.
  • Bar change feedback — a delta ≥ 2 flashes the fill and pins a small +N/−N chip on the value for ~1.2s; low bars (≤ 25) pulse their label.
  • Ticker — status lines swap in with a short slide-settle; priority chain unchanged (training → condition → unlock → growth hint → flavor).
  • Hover / press — buttons lift 1px with a warm keyline brighten on hover; press squashes 0.98. CTA idles with a soft glow when AP is unspent late in the day.
  • Reduced motion — every animation collapses to an opacity crossfade or nothing, honoring the saved setting and prefers-reduced-motion.
