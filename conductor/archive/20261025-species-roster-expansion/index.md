The roster grows from 6 to 9 species: three new lines (Fernstag / plant,
Reefclaw / water, Cindermoor / fire) join as ladder tier rewards with new
voxel archetypes, probe-tuned kits, full lifecycle parity, refreshed boss
pairings, and a retroactive grant for already-claimed tiers. Save stays v25.
