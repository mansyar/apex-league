### New builders in `fx/shot-geometry.ts`

- **`boulder` core** — a `Group` of fixed geometry only: a `DodecahedronGeometry`
  scaled `(1.15, 0.72, 1)` for a flat slab, plus one `TetrahedronGeometry` chip
  offset `(0.1, 0.09, -0.06)·size` and rotated `(0.6, 0.4, 0.2)`. Two shared
  `MeshBasicMaterial`s, **no halo, no `Math.random()` at build time**, so two
  identical shots are identical meshes (test: "the boulder is built
  deterministically" compares every vertex position of two builds).
- **`dust` trail** — four flat `TetrahedronGeometry(r * 0.9)` facets, distinct
  from shards (`Icosahedron`), seeds (`Octahedron`), and round droplets/orbs.
  Dust is the only family rendered with `THREE.NormalBlending`: it is grit, not
  light, which is what keeps the Earth lane non-luminous without touching
  `bursts.ts` (outside Touches). The `trailGeometry(family, r)` helper carries
  the four-way choice.
- Unions extended: `ShotLook["geometry"]` gains `"boulder"`,
  `ShotLook["trail"]` gains `"dust"`.
