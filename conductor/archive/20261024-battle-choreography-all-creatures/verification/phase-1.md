Signature comments, as authored above the table:

- `draconic`: quick and light — fast stride, eager lean, subtle coil sway;
  snaps out of a short windup.
- `bovine`: heavy — big slow bob, deep breathing, the heaviest flinch;
  coils slow and deep, then commits the whole body.
- `axolotl`: fluid — soft gait, the strongest undulating idle sway; gives
  softly on the commit and absorbs the recoil.
- `storm_hound`: wired — a twitchy fast stride and a jittery idle; barely
  winds up, then bucks hard off the release.
- `falcon`: light and precise — almost no bob, a still exact stance; dives
  from a short windup and rides the recoil out.
- `tortoise`: ponderous and immovable — a slow low gait, deep slow breath;
  barely flinches, winds up forever, and commits the shell.

The three pre-existing archetypes (`draconic`, `bovine`, `axolotl`) keep
their nine body values byte-identical to the base revision and gain the
three attack knobs; no recorded defect, no before→after correction.

Per-knob behaviour proofs:

- **Windup** (`applyPosture`): `pitch`, `roll`, `rise`, `coilX` amplitudes
  multiplied by `ao.profile.windupMult`; `half = sin(wπ/2)` and
  `full = sin(wπ)` untouched; signature unchanged.
- **Recoil** (`applyPosture`): `pitchOut`, `sweepX` (both terms), `riseOut`
  amplitudes multiplied by `ao.profile.recoilMult`; phase fractions
  untouched; signature unchanged.
- **Strike** (`strikePose`, consumed by `meleeStep`): coil, drop, lift,
  lean, roll, commit impulse and settle impulse all scale by
  `ao.profile.strikeMult`; `along` (the arrival point) deliberately does
  not. The victim's reaction block in `melee.ts` (`contactReaction`,
  dodge dash, `snapTo`) is unchanged, so a strike still lands where it
  always landed and the reaction reads its own profile.

## Purity proof

- `choreo.ts` contains **no imports at all**: `^import|from "|require(|document|window|globalThis|three`
  matches nothing in the file. It is data plus pure functions — no THREE,
  no DOM, no globals.
- The table is frozen at module load: `Object.freeze(PROFILES)` followed by
  `for (const p of Object.values(PROFILES)) Object.freeze(p)` at top level
  (L186–188), with `DEFAULT_PROFILE = Object.freeze(NEUTRAL)`. The
  "profiles are frozen" test asserts `Object.isFrozen` on every resolved
  profile, including the unknown-id fallback.
- Only the existing posture/strike entry points changed; `applyPosture`'s
  exported signature and `meleeStep`'s `StepRunner` signature are
  unchanged, and `strikePose` is a new pure function with no side effects.
- **Reduced motion still wins:** every new impulse flows through
  `impulse(io, c, amount)`, which damps to 0.4× when `io.reduced()`; the
  driver keeps zeroing gait/breath/sway with `life = 0`. No new animation
  channel bypasses either gate.
- **No timing change:** `PLAYBACK_PHASES`, `CONTACT_T`,
  `PROJECTILE_LIFE_MS`, `PROJECTILE_ARC` and the reaction `slide`/`dash`
  values are untouched — only amplitudes move. Distances are untouched:
  `strikePose`'s `along` reproduces the base `-0.22`, `+reach`,
  `reach·(1−easeOut)` sequence, and `STANCE_GAP`/`CONTACT_GAP`/`MIN_CLOSE`
  keep their values (all pre-existing geometry tests still pass unchanged).
- `packages/sim` and `packages/voxel` are untouched; `SAVE_VERSION` stays

**Independent behaviour proof (the load-bearing check for a choreography change)**

The phase rewrote `steps/melee.ts` from hand-inlined offsets into a pure
`strikePose()` in `steps/pure.ts`, and threaded two multipliers through
`applyPosture()`. A behaviour drift here would be a silent animation regression,
so the primary wrote a throwaway differential (never committed, deleted after the
run) comparing `strikePose(...)` at `strikeMult = 1` against the pre-track body
formulas — the same numbers the phase base hardcoded — over
`w ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.13, 0.3, 0.3001, 0.46, 0.6, 0.6001, 0.77, 0.94, 1}` for all three
beats, at a 1.7-unit reach:

| Claim | Result |
|---|---|
| Windup: `along`, `y`, `roll` equal the old `-0.22·sin`, `-0.05·sin`, `0.05·sin`; the beat owns no pitch and no impulse | equal to 12 decimal places |
| Contact: `along` = `-0.22 + (reach + 0.22)·easeOutCubic(w)` (algebraically identical to the new `reach·e − coil·(1−e)`); `y`, `pitch`, `roll` equal; the commit impulse fires only past `w > 0.3` | equal to 12 dp, impulse gate identical |
| Recovery: `along` = `reach·(1 − easeOut(w))`; lean/roll equal; the settle impulse fires only on a hit past `w > 0.6`; height untouched | equal to 12 dp, both gates identical |
| The arrival point is invariant under the weight (a distance, not an amplitude) | `along → reach` exactly at `w = 1` for both weights |
| A miss never settles | no impulse on any `w` |
| A heavier weight scales `y`, `pitch`, `roll` linearly (×1.5 ⇒ ×1.5) | exact |
