## Explicit evidence

### Legacy golden (byte-identical pre-v19 numbers)

`evolveCreature` on a `branch: "legacy"` creature applies `+{hp:3, atk:2, def:2, spd:1}` at the juvenile gate and `+{hp:6, atk:4, def:4, spd:2}` at the champion gate, so a champion ends at `+{hp:9, atk:6, def:6, spd:3}` relative to its rolled base — the exact deltas the pre-change implementation produced. `EVOLUTION_STAT_BOOST` and `CHAMPION_STAT_BOOST` are now identity aliases of `LEGACY_JUVENILE_BOOST` / `LEGACY_CHAMPION_BOOST` in `branches.ts`, so the public constants keep their names and the numbers have a single home without an `evolution.ts` ↔ `branches.ts` cycle.

## Notes

- No save schema, migration, client, or documentation changes: `SAVE_VERSION` stays 18. A v18 save loads a creature with `branch === undefined`; `evolveCreature` and the loadout helpers read `creature.branch ?? null`, so a pre-v19 juvenile falls back to the legacy tables at the champion gate and never re-derives. Phase 2 owns persistence.
- Fixture discipline: the evolution helpers (`scenarioState`, `gateCreature`) and the direct `createCreature` evolution fixtures in `evolution.test.ts` default to `branch: "legacy"` so their pre-v19 assertions stay numerically unchanged; new branch tests opt in with `branch: null` or an explicit branch id.
- The derivation rule exists once (`branchLean`); `evolveCreature` only calls it. No picker, state flag, or offer was introduced.
- Rework: `defaultLoadout` previously sliced `knownMoves`, which ends with the signature, so a branched juvenile below six trained days defaulted to three kit moves plus its signature — that violated AC-6. It now reuses `signatureMoveOf` (no duplicated branch check) and drops that id from the default slice; baby, legacy, and six-day defaults are byte-identical to before. Regression coverage lives in `loadout.test.ts › branch signature moves` (3-day branched juvenile, 6-day branched juvenile, 3-day legacy juvenile).
- Verification numbers above are from the rework run; the phase is squashed into one commit on this branch.
