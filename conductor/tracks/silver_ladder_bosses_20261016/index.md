# Track: Silver Ladder & Rival Bosses (`silver_ladder_bosses_20261016`)

- **Type:** Feature (Phase 2 — League Depth)
- **Status:** New
- **Branch:** `track/silver_ladder_bosses_20261016` (base: `main @ 2d4d69b`)

Open the door the Bronze ladder left ajar: points banked past Bronze I make
the next entered cup the **Promotion Cup**, whose final rival is the first
authored boss. Winning enters Silver III; Silver's three sub-tiers climb
automatically on points (thresholds probe-tuned). Two further authored bosses
mark the journey — a non-blocking checkpoint rival at Silver III and a
recurring apex rival at Silver I. Bosses are pinned builds of existing
archetypes (fixed seed, absolute stats, hand-authored kit + tactic, coach
persona, lower-third intro) — no new voxel content. Boss defeats persist in
a save ledger (v11) and surface as callouts, coach lines, and Hall of Fame
scalps. No demotion, no decay.
