## Calibration methodology (recorded for the budgets)

A temporary calibration harness (since deleted by the primary in `3e9334c`) swept 200 seeds × 3 stages per archetype through `measureRatiosFromFields` and probed seeds 0–59 at full grid for boundary and zero-voxel defects. Measured spreads (final): falcon legs 0.43–1.0, neck 0.05–0.56, head 0.09–0.71, body 1.18–2.43; tortoise legs 0.33–0.78, neck 0.03–0.41, head 0.09–0.71, body 1.62–3.57. Defects found and fixed en route: signature parts double-adding the grid origin (`at()` on already grid-space coordinates → pivots at z ≈ 58, zero voxels), falcon tailTip buried inside the tail box under first-wins paint order, tortoise neck swallowed by shell+body, tortoise plastron clipping the y=0 voxel-center floor for bg < 1, and tortoise legs fully hidden by the low torso on the audit lattice (body raised to `7.8*bg + 2`).
