## What changed

The game state now holds the **stable**: every pet the player owns that is
not in the ranch. `GameState` gains `stable: Creature[]` — no capacity limit,
exactly one active pet (`state.creature`). The night carries the stable
across untouched, so a stashed pet is **frozen**: it neither decays nor ages,
its illness cooldown and training assignment freeze with it, and it resumes
exactly where it left off.

The **swap seam** is two pure functions in a new module
`packages/sim/src/stable.ts`: `sendToStable(state)` parks the ranch pet and
`bringToRanch(state, creatureId)` hands the ranch to a stashed pet and files
the pet that held it. The swap is **free** (no action point, no day),
**instant** and **lossless** — every field of a pet travels with it — and it
consumes no randomness, so no seeded stream moves. Both seams refuse while a
cup or spar is live (`state.phase === "cup"`), the same day-phase lock the
rest of the game uses, and the last pet the player owns cannot be sent away.

The **Storm Gift** now hatches into the stable: the hound joins the reserve
and the partner the player already had stays exactly as it was — it never
left the ranch, so its career is never retired and it is not in the Hall of
Fame either. A stashed gift pet counts as hatched, so the one-gift rule
still holds.

A **graduation** may hand the ranch to a stashed pet
(`graduateFromStable(state, creatureId)` in `packages/sim/src/legacy.ts`): the
retiring champion is archived to the Hall of Fame exactly as before, the
successor keeps every field it had, and it is not added to the Hall a second
time. **Breeding stays Hall-only** — the pool reads the Hall and the retiring
champion, never the stable — and the cups-won tally stays the family's.

The persisted shape moved to **save v24** (the second linear step of this
track, after v23's per-pet age): the `23:` migration stamps an empty stable
(a v23 save predates the stable and holds exactly one pet — the ranch pet),
the validation refuses a malformed stable (every entry must be a whole pet:
a known species, a positive age, a valid stage, branch and loadout), and
`docs/save-schema.md` documents the field, the migration step and the rule.

### The swap seam's contract (P2.T2)

`packages/sim/src/stable.ts` is the whole surface the client needs:

| Export | Contract |
|---|---|
| `sendToStable(state)` | Parks the ranch pet at the end of the stable. Refuses `{ok:false, reason:"cup-phase"}` during a live cup, `"no-creature"` when the ranch is empty and `"only-pet"` when the player owns no other pet. Free, instant, no RNG. |
| `bringToRanch(state, creatureId)` | Hands the ranch to the named stable pet and files the pet that held it. Refuses `"cup-phase"` and `"unknown-pet"`. Free, instant, no RNG. |
| `stablePetId(index)` | The stable slot's identity: `"stable:0"`, `"stable:3"`. A pet carries no id of its own, so the slot is what the modal hands back. |
| `stablePetSlot(creatureId)` | The inverse: `"stable:2"` → `2`, anything else → `null`. |
| `StableReason` / `StableResult` | `"cup-phase" \| "no-creature" \| "only-pet" \| "unknown-pet"` and `{ok:true, state} \| {ok:false, reason}`. |

The client composes the two seams into the swap that always leaves exactly
one pet in the ranch: the swap is *send the ranch pet, then bring the chosen
pet back* — or, in the reverse direction, *bring the chosen pet, then send the
outgoing pet*.

## Notes

- **The freeze is structural, not a rule.** A stashed pet is frozen because
  the night settles only the active creature (`packages/sim/src/ticker.ts`),
  the training and care seams act on `state.creature`, the evolution gate
  reads the settled pet, and the breeding pool reads the Hall. No new
  "is stashed" checks were scattered through the sim.
- **The save is now v24, the second linear step of this track** (v23 was the
  per-pet age). Every older save still migrates forward cleanly: the `23:`
  step stamps an empty stable and forwards everything else untouched — the
  active pet keeps its own age, the lineage, the ladder, the settings and a
  live cup session all survive.
- **The validation is one predicate.** `isValidSaveState` now requires
  `stable` to be a list whose every entry passes `isValidCreature`, so a
  stashed pet is validated exactly as strictly as the ranch pet: a known
  species, a positive `ageDays`, a valid stage, branch and loadout, a
  well-formed kit. `isValidCreature` gained the missing **loadout** check
  (a list of move ids, or absent) so the rule covers the whole pet rather
  than half of it. A half-written stable fails the load; it does not half-kit
  a pet.
- **The only-pet rule.** `sendToStable` refuses with `"only-pet"` when the
  player owns no other pet, which is the spec's edge case *"sending the only
  pet to stable is impossible (a swap always leaves exactly one in the
  ranch)"*. With two or more pets the ranch may be momentarily empty — the
  composed swap (send, then bring) always leaves exactly one pet active.
- **The gift's marker now spans three places.** `giftEggHatched` counts the
  active creature, **the stable** and the Hall of Fame, so a stashed gift pet
  counts as hatched and the one-gift rule holds.
- **A graduation is not a career event for the successor.** The handover
  raises the generation exactly as a normal graduation does, archives the
  retiree exactly as before, and leaves the successor out of the Hall — it is
  not a retired pet.
- **The cups tally is untouched by everything in this phase** — the swap, the
  gift hatch and the handover all leave `cupsWon` alone; prestige and the
  champion title stay lineage measures.
- **Phase 3 handoff (the client surface).** The client needs, from
  `@apex/sim`: `sendToStable`, `bringToRanch`, `stablePetId`, `stablePetSlot`,
  `StableReason`, `StableResult` (all exported from the barrel), plus the
  `state.stable` list on the state. The "Bring to the ranch" button calls
  `bringToRanch(state, stablePetId(index))`; the day-phase lock surfaces as
  `reason === "cup-phase"`, which is where the coaching copy goes. The age
  readout uses each stashed pet's own `ageDays`.
- **Phase 3 handoff (the gift card).** `apps/client/src/app/hud-screen.ts`
  still focuses `.creature-head` after a hatch — correct, because the ranch
  pet is still the partner, but the **gift card** and the nursery copy now
  describe a pet that waits in the stable rather than one that takes the
  ranch. The client's gift copy lives in `apps/client/src/gift.ts` and
  `apps/client/src/hud/copy.ts`.
- **The phase commits are task-level and land in the plan's order**; the
  primary squashes them into the phase commit on acceptance. Each commit is
  self-consistent (the behaviour change and the test that pins it land
  together); the suite was run green on the final state.
