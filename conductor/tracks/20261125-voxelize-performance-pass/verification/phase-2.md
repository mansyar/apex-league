## Techniques

### Voxelizer

- Emit each voxel and its class immediately when the first field claims it,
  eliminating the owner/class grids and second full-grid emission scan.
- Reuse one mutable sample point across cells.
- Preserve cell-center coordinates, `sdf(p) <= 0` inclusion, first-claim ownership,
  callback/classification order, and x→y→z output order.

### SDF

- Replace temporary `Vec3` helper allocations with scalar arithmetic.
- Hoist round-cone constants and fallback selection while preserving the original
  floating-point operation order.
- Recompute capsule segment invariants per call to preserve mutable-input
  behavior; leave `smoothUnion` child evaluation order unchanged.

### Mesher/AO

- Replace repeated per-part slice scans and per-slice mask allocations with one
  shared visible-face scan per axis/slice.
- Record the face owner, direction, class, and packed AO signature in reusable
  slice masks; each cell pair can produce at most one visible face.
- Use direct axis-stride indexing and emit into per-part buffers while preserving
  each part's axis→slice→(u,v) order.
- Preserve greedy rectangle selection, direction/class/AO merge boundaries,
  corner occlusion, negative-face AO reversal, darker-diagonal splitting, winding,
  and exact arrays. `ao.ts` is unchanged.

## Deviations and resolved blockers

- `packages/voxel/scripts/benchmark-voxelize.test.ts` was the only tracked file
  outside the brief's `Touches` list. Its pre-existing assertions called
  `.toThrow(...)` on the return value of `summarizeSamples(...)` rather than on a
  function, causing the required package typecheck to fail. The smallest fix
  wraps each call in `expect(() => summarizeSamples(...)).toThrow(...)`; expected
  errors and coverage are unchanged, and no benchmark behavior was weakened.
- Several exact benchmark, Biome, package-gate, and scratch-capture invocations
  were denied by the subagent shell permission layer. The primary ran the exact
  commands unchanged; successful primary results and raw evidence supersede the
  denials. No permission-denial workaround was used.
- Biome formatting diagnostics in characterization tests, the benchmark test,
  and the mesher were repaired without semantic or expected-output changes.
  Unused destructuring and two `let` bindings were corrected, and expressions
  were reformatted. Final required and extended Biome checks pass.

  by the shared-slice implementation; no experimental code remains.
